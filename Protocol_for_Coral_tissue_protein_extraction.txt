Protein Extraction Protocol from Coral tissue samples

Equipment Required:

•	Vacuum freeze dryer (or Lyophilizer) (Operon Co., Ltd. Model: FDB-5502)
•	Table top Centrifuge (Eppendorf, 5424R)
•	Mixmate vortex mixer
•	Bath-sonicator (Elmasonic S 15 (H))
•	SpeedVac Concentrator (Thermo Scientific Savant SPD1010)
•	Homogenizer (Cole-Parmer, Spex HG-200)
•	3 kDa MWCO filter (P/N: UFC500324, Millipore Amicon)
•	Pierce™ C18 Spin Columns (P/N 89870, Thermo Scientific)

Chemicals Required:

•	High purity (LC/MS-grade, A456-4) Methanol 
•	Urea (P/N: U5378, Sigma-Aldrich)
•	Ammonium Bicarbonate (A6141-500G, Sigma-Aldrich)
•	Dithiothreitol, DTT (P/N: 43819-5G, Sigma-Aldrich)
•	Iodoacetamide, IAA (P/N: I1149-25G, Sigma-Aldrich)
•	Formic acid (P/N: A117, Fischer Chemical)
•	MS-grade trypsin (P/N: T6567-5X20UG, Merck

Sample Preparation

•	Tissue sample crushed to powder under liquid nitrogen
•	Lyophilize the samples for completely dried powder (16 hours)
•	Weigh 50–100 mg (typically 50 mg) of powdered tissue and transfer into bead rupture tubes containing beads 
•	Add 300 µL homogenizing buffer (8 M urea in 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate buffer). 
•	Homogenize using a bead rupture for 3 cycles at 2000 rpm (10 seconds shaking followed by 15 seconds rest)
•	Transfer the homogenate into new MCT (micro centrifuge tube) 
•	Vortex the homogenate with urea buffer for 10 minutes at 4⁰C
•	Sonicate in water bath sonicator for 5 minutes on ice
•	Repeat the steps 7 and 8 for total of 3 cycles 
•	Check for the presence of any slimy threads. If present, repeat the steps 7 and 8 for 2 more times or until the solution becomes clear without its presence.
•	Centrifuge the lysate at 16,000 × g for 10 minutes at 4°C
•	Carefully separate the supernatant and transfer into a new microcentrifuge tube
•	Desalting and buffer exchange using 3 kDa MWCO Centrifugal Filter
•	Determine the protein concentration of the supernatant using BCA Protein Assay Kit

Protein Digestion
•	Transfer the supernatant corresponding to 25µg protein into a new microcentrifuge tube.
•	Add 2 µl of 100 mM Dithiothreitol, (DTT) to protein sample
•	Incubate at 60°C for 30 min in vortex mixer
•	Add 2 µl of 200 mM iodoacetamide (IAA).
•	Incubate in the dark at room temperature for 30 min.
•	Add 3 µl of trypsin.  (Trypsin prepared at a concentration of 0.4 µg/ µL in 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate)
•	Incubate at 37°C overnight with gentle shaking.
•	Add 1 µl of 1% formic acid to stop the reaction.
•	Incubate at 37°C for 20 min in vortex mixer
•	Digested peptides are cleaned using C18 spin column
•	Resuspend the peptides in 30uL of 0.1% formic acid
•	Vortex and transfer to LC-MS vials
